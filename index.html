Taste the Tradition

Welcome to Le Mandingue, your local home for authentic West African and Liberian comfort food. Every dish we serve is crafted with traditional, homemade spices, fresh ingredients, and a passion for flavor. From our signature smoky Jollof Rice to our slow-cooked Cassava Leaf, we bring the vibrant tastes of the DMV's West African community straight to your plate.

Our Specialties

Signature Rice Plates

Jollof Rice

Rich, smoky, perfectly spiced African rice served with your choice of grilled chicken, fried fish, or tender Dibi (lamb).

Cassava Leaf with Rice

Rich and savory Liberian-style greens slow-cooked to perfection, served over a bed of steaming white rice.

Potato Greens

Fresh, tender greens traditionally prepared with rich palm oil and seasoned assorted proteins.

Grilled & Smoked Specialties

Dibi Lamb

Succulent, smoky grilled lamb seasoned with authentic West African spices, served sliced with grilled onions.

Attéké & Fish

Traditional Ivorian steamed cassava grain paired with perfectly fried or grilled whole fish and savory sides.

Traditional Soups & Sides

Egusi Soup with Fufu

Rich, hearty soup thickened with ground melon seeds and leafy greens, served with smooth, traditional fufu.

Snacks & Drinks

Sweet Fried Plantains, Kala (Puff-Puff), refreshing homemade Ginger Juice, and Hibiscus (Bissap) drinks.
