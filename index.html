Taste the Tradition

Welcome to Le Mandingue, your local home for authentic West African and Liberian comfort food. Every single plate we craft features rich, complex flavors built from traditional homemade spice rubs, premium slow-simmered ingredients, and structural culinary mastery.

Whether you're craving the deep, comforting texture of authentic Liberian-style Cassava leaf or the flawless sear on our signature smoky charcoal Dibi Lamb, we invite you to experience the absolute best of regional African cooking in the DMV area.
